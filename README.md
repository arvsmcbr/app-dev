# My favorite movies: The Karate Kid (2010)
## Description
Karate Kid is a 1984 American martial arts drama film directed by John G. Avildsen and written by Robert Mark Kamen. It is the first film in The Karate Kid franchise. The film stars Ralph Macchio, Pat Morita, Elisabeth Shue and William Zabka.[4][5] The story follows Daniel LaRusso (Macchio), an Italian-American teenager from New Jersey who moves with his widowed mother to the Reseda neighborhood of Los Angeles. There, LaRusso encounters harassment from bullies, one of whom is Johnny Lawrence (Zabka), the ex-boyfriend of LaRusso's love interest, Ali Mills (Shue). LaRusso is taught karate by handyman and war veteran Mr. Miyagi (Morita) to help LaRusso defend himself and compete in a karate tournament against his bullies.

## What I like about this movies:
I like this movie because it teaches us the importance of courage, discipline, and never giving up. I also enjoy the friendship between Daniel and Mr. Miyagi because they show the value of trust and respect. My favorite part is watching Daniel overcome his fears and become more confident through karate.
